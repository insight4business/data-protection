# Data Protection

Classification, DLP, privacy (LGPD/GDPR), retention, insider risk, encryption, breach response, third-party privacy and data inventory.

_Templates in this folder are generic and vendor-neutral. All examples are fictional. Numbers (SLAs, thresholds, periods) are examples only._

## Sub-processes

| Sub-process | Use it when | Files |
|---|---|---|
| [breach-response/](breach-response/README.md) | A personal data breach is suspected or confirmed and you must decide on notification. | 1 |
| [classification-and-labeling/](classification-and-labeling/README.md) | You are defining how data is classified and labeled. | 2 |
| [data-inventory-and-masking/](data-inventory-and-masking/README.md) | You are inventorying data stores and deciding what must be masked or protected. | 1 |
| [dlp/](dlp/README.md) | You are designing, rolling out or operating data loss prevention. | 5 |
| [encryption-and-key-management/](encryption-and-key-management/README.md) | You are standardising encryption and key lifecycle. | 1 |
| [insider-risk-and-ediscovery/](insider-risk-and-ediscovery/README.md) | You are monitoring insider risk or responding to a legal hold or eDiscovery request. | 2 |
| [privacy-lgpd-gdpr/](privacy-lgpd-gdpr/README.md) | You are handling personal data: processing records, DPIAs, data subject requests, consent, transfers or regulation mapping. | 9 |
| [retention-and-log-architecture/](retention-and-log-architecture/README.md) | You are setting how long records and logs are kept, where, and how they are disposed of. | 3 |
| [third-party-privacy/](third-party-privacy/README.md) | You are assessing a processor or other third party that handles personal data. | 1 |

## How to use

1. Find the sub-process you are in, open its README.
2. Copy the template you need and replace every `<PLACEHOLDER>`.
3. Review with your own legal and security teams before adopting.
